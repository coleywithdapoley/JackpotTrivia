# Privacy Policy — If You Know, You Win

**Effective date:** September 2, 2026

This policy covers the iOS app **If You Know, You Win** (internal name Jackpot Trivia).

Questions or deletion requests: **cobb.cole@gmail.com**

## What the app does

If You Know, You Win is a Detroit-themed trivia game. You can try a short sample without an account. An email account is required to play the official daily round, save scores, and appear on standings.

There are **no in-app purchases** in this version. Points and XP are bragging rights only and have **no cash value**.

## Information we collect

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Email address | Create and sign in to your account |
| Display name | Show you on standings and in challenges |
| Account ID | Keep your scores, progress, and settings together |
| Gameplay (scores, streaks, XP, categories played) | Run daily rounds, practice, and standings |
| Content you submit (question reports, challenge details) | Fix questions and run friend challenges |

We do **not** sell your information. We do **not** use it for third-party advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps. We do **not** collect payment information.

Passwords are handled by our account provider (Supabase). We do not store your password in the app.

## Where it is stored

Account and standings data is stored with **Supabase** (cloud hosting, United States) when the app is configured for online play. Some settings and recent scores also stay on your device.

**Standings are visible to other players** who use the app. Your display name and scores can appear on Today, Week, and All Time boards.

## Who can see admin tools

Admin tools (catalog edits, access settings) are limited to founder accounts. Regular testers and players do not see them.

## Children

The app is intended for people **17 and older**. The optional Private Lounge includes mature (PG-13 / 18+) trivia. Do not use the app if you are under 17.

## Your choices

- You can play the guest sample without creating an account.
- You can stop using the app at any time.
- To **delete your account and associated scores**, email **cobb.cole@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with. We will remove your account data from our systems within a reasonable time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
